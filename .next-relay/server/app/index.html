How it works

Six stages, one screen each. Every stage is visible and correctable.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    PDFs, scans, Word, Excel, images. Per-page OCR fallback for scanned or broken-font pages.

  2. 02

    Triage

    Your plain-English rules decide what's in scope, so irrelevant attachments never reach extraction.

  3. 03

    Extract

    A local LLM fills your fields, with a confidence score and a reason for every blank.

  4. 04

    Review

    Only uncertain fields need a human. Side-by-side original, keyboard-driven approve & next.

  5. 05

    Export

    One standardized Excel/CSV per project: approved rows, your columns, your labels.

  6. 06

    Fulfil

    Turn an approved request into a task: invite vendors, draft emails, track delivery and invoice.
